# The patrol loop

How the drive works from the first stop on: one closed path that the car drives round for ever (Phase 8a/8b, replacing the old "end of the shift" reset and the "New patrol" button).

## What the visitor sees

The patrol is a loop, not a road with two ends:

1. **Out.** The car starts at Ignition and drives out along the road in its right-hand lane, past every stop, to The Forks.
2. **The Forks roundabout.** Driving on past The Forks, the car goes into the roundabout, once round it, and out onto the return lane heading back to the city. There is no button: it is just driving on. On the roundabout the car drives itself (steady pace, an ease in and out), so Brake and Turn around do nothing there.
3. **Back.** The car drives the whole route in reverse, in the lane on the other side of the road (Canada drives on the right). Every stop is visited again, in reverse order, **except** The Forks (the car has just been round it) and Ignition (it is only where the loop turns the car round). Signs and landmarks light up as the car approaches them from either side, and the stations open from this side too: the red light is on the other side of the stop, with its own signal mast, and the car parks from the other lane.
4. **The turnaround.** Behind Ignition a small roundabout (one lap is about five seconds) turns the car round and puts it back on the outbound lane. The patrol loops for ever.

Driving rules:

- **Forward input always means keep driving the way the car is heading**: scroll down, swipe up, ArrowDown or ArrowRight, Space, Page Down, the Next stop button.
- **Backward input brakes.** Scroll up, swipe down, ArrowUp or ArrowLeft, Page Up, Shift+Space and the Brake button all bring the car to a smooth stop. The car **never reverses and never turns round by itself**: jittery back-and-forth scrolling can only start and stop it. Once it has braked, a hint says "Stopped. To drive the other way, press Turn around."
- **Turn around** is a deliberate U-turn in the middle of the road (about 1.8 seconds, a hairpin into the other lane, a few metres further on in the old direction). The car then stands facing the other way until driven on. It is not offered on a roundabout, in a station, or while starting over.
- **Start over** fades the view to black (800 ms), cuts back to the first stop heading out, and fades in. It needs no lap and replaces the old "New patrol" button.
- **The route map** jumps to a stop by the shorter way: a stop has two visits (outbound and heading back), and the car either keeps driving forward round the loop or turns round **once** and drives the short way. The map shows an arrow on "you are here" (down the line is outbound, up is heading back) and the heading in its title.
- **The status line** says where you are and which way: "Stop 7 of 11: Millennium Library · outbound" or "· heading back", and "En route · outbound" between stops.
- **Radio captions.** Heading back, a stop uses its `radioReturn` line from `src/content/stops.ts` if it has one (Dispatch: "Unit 02, heading back to base."), otherwise the outbound line is reused. The three outbound lines that say "on your left" (the campus, the warehouse, City services) each have a return line that says "on your right", because those buildings are on the other side coming back (`TODO(content)`: owner to tweak the wording). The call that starts the patrol ("…starting patrol.", `firstLapOnly`) is only said on the first lap, and "New patrol starting." no longer exists. The loading screen is never replayed.
- **Reduced motion:** the roundabout laps, the turnaround and the U-turn are cuts, never continuous motion. Scrolling and the buttons work as usual.

## How it works

One closed path, measured in metres of driving `s`. The car's whole state is `progress.s` (it counts up lap after lap and the loop wraps it); route position `t`, the direction and the pose are all worked out from it, so nothing can disagree.

Rules worth knowing:

- Driving never goes backwards: the step takes the larger of the car's place and the eased one. Braking pulls the request back to the car (plus a short coast), the car eases to a stop.
- The magnet holds the car at each stop and each station light, in both directions. A stop has two magnet points on the loop; a light is `offset` metres before its stop along the way the car is going, so heading back it is on the other side of the stop.
- A jump or Turn around while the car is in a station backs it out first (the existing pending-move rule); brake while parked just backs out.
- Film grain: `grainOpacity(sunrise)` in `src/lib/quality.ts` is 0.22 at night and 0.05 at the full sunrise. The atmosphere follows the route position, so heading back the sunrise fades back to night.

## Seeing where the car goes

`?probe=on` makes the scene write the car's position to `document.body.dataset.car` ("x,z,heading") every frame, so an end-to-end test can follow it (`src/scene/CarProbe.tsx`, `src/lib/probe.ts`). Nothing else changes, and visitors never see it.

## Tests

- `loopRoute.test.ts`: the pieces and their seams, wrapping, road position and direction on every piece, mirroring and landing a U-turn, gates, the visits (every stop out, all but the first and last back), light points both sides of a stop, the magnet (flat at a point, monotonic round the seam, unwrapped), the next visit and the shortest-way plan (never two U-turns).
- `roundabout.test.ts`: both roundabouts (centred on the road axis, tangent join, once round with the island on the left, out onto the other lane, smooth every 25 cm, ring strips, bank with its open mouth).
- `loop.test.ts`: the content numbers match the geometry; the whole loop is one closed path with no jump in position or heading, seam included; each stop is driven in its own lane both ways.
- `drive.test.ts`, `drive.loop.test.ts`, `drive.station.test.ts`, `drive.menu.test.ts`: the store, and a frame-by-frame simulation of the car (`stepLoop` and the same U-turn landing): **forward input alone drives a full loop and comes back outbound with no turns and no reversing**; **jittery back-and-forth input never turns the car round**; **Turn around turns it round exactly once**; brake holds; nothing brakes on a roundabout; reduced motion cuts through both roundabouts; jumps turn round at most once; Start over.
- Component tests for `DriveControls`, `Subtitles`, `ShiftFade`, `RouteMap`, `StationControls` and `useDriveInput`.
- `tests/e2e/loop.spec.ts` (with `?probe=on`, following the car by where it is, not by the clock): past The Forks the car goes round the ring and comes back on the return lane; all the way round (Dispatch heading back with its own line, round the turnaround, out again at the same pose, no "starting patrol" the second time); Start over; a station opening from the way back; and the reduced-motion cut. `tests/e2e/uturn.spec.ts`: Turn around turns the car round once, smoothly, into the other lane, never in reverse; scrolling back only brakes.
