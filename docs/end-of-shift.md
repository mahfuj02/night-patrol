# The end of the shift

How the drive ends and starts again (Phase 7a and 7c): the roundabout at The Forks, then the reset to the first stop.

## What the visitor sees

1. The car reaches The Forks (the last stop). The Contact station can be opened and left as usual. The drive ends here: the road runs 38 m further, but the car cannot be driven past the stop.
2. With the car out of the station, the Next button at the last stop reads **New patrol ›** (it is the same button, so its place in the tab order and its focus do not change).
3. Pressing it starts the end of the shift. The caption **"New patrol starting."** appears in the dispatch radio region (screen readers read it there), and the car **loops the roundabout**: it drives straight along the 38 m lead-in in its right-hand lane, bears right to join the ring tangentially, goes once round it with the island on its left (Canada drives on the right and circulates counter-clockwise), leaves it onto the other lane of the same road heading **back toward the city**, and runs a little way back along the road. The camera follows behind it the whole way. The lap takes about ten seconds. Nothing else can drive the car meanwhile, and the Open button is not offered.
4. With the car back on the road, the view fades to black (800 ms). While it is dark the car is put back at the first stop with a **cut**: the scene snaps its progress to the start, so it never drives back along the whole route. Any station state and pending station open are cleared.
5. The view fades back in (800 ms) at the first stop, and the drive controls say "Stop 1 of 11: Ignition".

Under **reduced motion** there is no lap and no veil at all: the reset runs at once and the view cuts. A lap the scene never finishes (a very slow machine) is ended after 70 s so the shift cannot hang.

Scrolling back mid-route does not reset; that is the Phase 8 U-turn.

## How it works

| Part        | Where                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| State       | `shift` (`idle`, `looping`, `fading-out`, `fading-in`) and `resetCount` in `src/store/drive.ts`; `startNewPatrol` (only at the last stop, with the car out of its station), `finishLoop`, `completeFadeOut` (the cut) and `finishReset`. While `shift` is not idle every drive action and `openStation` are ignored. The drive ends at `LAST_STOP_T`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| The ring    | `src/scene/Road/roundabout.ts` (pure geometry: `roundaboutLayout`, `loopPath`, `ringStrips`, `bankGeometry`, `ROUNDABOUT`) and `RoundaboutRoad.tsx` (the ring as road strips with the road's own surface, a snowy island, and a low bank that is open where the road meets the ring; three meshes). The road runs straight on 38 m past The Forks stop (two extra control points in `route.ts`) and meets the ring head-on, square to it: the ring is centred on the road's axis, straight ahead of the end of the road, and its outer edge starts exactly where the road ends. The ridges, lamps and trees run on to the ring because they follow the road |
| The lap     | `loopPath` is five stretches: the lead-in in the car's lane, a curve bearing right onto the ring, once round the ring, a curve off it into the other lane, and the run back along the road. `RoundaboutDriver.tsx` moves the car along it through the same car and camera override the station arrival uses (`stationView`), with a short ease at each end, and ends the lap; the car holds its last pose behind the veil                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| The fade    | `src/ui/Hud/ShiftFade.tsx`: a dark veil with no text and no pointer events; its timers (`SHIFT_FADE_MS`, `LOOP_MAX_MS` in `src/lib/shift.ts`) step the state, and under reduced motion skip the lap                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| The cut     | `ProgressDriver` snaps `progress.t` and `CameraRig` snaps the camera when `resetCount` changes, so the view never glides back from the roundabout through the fade                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| The button  | `DriveControls`: Next becomes New patrol at the last stop (`drive.newPatrol` in `ui.ts`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| The caption | The `new-patrol` line in `radio.ts` (stop `ignition`), shown by `Subtitles` whenever `shift` is not idle (`TODO(content)`: owner to tweak the wording)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

## Film grain

The film grain's strength is `grainOpacity(sunrise)` in `src/lib/quality.ts`: 0.22 at night (it was 0.35) easing down to 0.05 at the full sunrise, because grain shows most on bright, flat snow. `Effects.tsx` writes it each frame from the atmosphere.

## Seeing where the car goes

`?probe=on` makes the scene write the car's position to `document.body.dataset.car` ("x,z,heading") every frame, so an end-to-end test can follow it (`src/scene/CarProbe.tsx`, `src/lib/probe.ts`). Nothing else changes, and visitors never see it.
