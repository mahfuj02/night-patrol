# The Millennium Library (Projects station)

The Projects station is the browse hub for projects. The car parks in front of the library, and a panel lists every published project as a book, with a catalog to find one and a book view with the details. This is Phase 5e, part 1: the building and the whole interface. The 3D interior (the push-in, shelves, reading table) is part 2 and is not built yet; see the plan in [navigation-redesign.md](navigation-redesign.md#projects-station-the-millennium-library).

## What the visitor gets

- **The building.** A stylized silhouette of the downtown glass front: a wide glazed block under a pale roof slab, end piers, an entrance canopy and plain lettering "Millennium Library" (from `signage.ts`; no logo, rule 10). It stands on the left, about 600 m along the route, on the first straight after Portage & Main.
- **Arrival.** The usual station pattern: a red light and an "Open Projects" button, then the car turns into the bay and parks. The lit wall shows a stack of titles (call number and name), as a picture only.
- **The panel** (side panel on a wide screen, bottom sheet on a phone), real DOM:
  - **Catalog:** a labelled search box (matches name, call number, shelf, tagline and stack, every word in any order), the shelf filters (All, Web, Mobile, Systems, AI) as toggle buttons, and a live count ("7 books").
  - **Shelf:** the matching books grouped by shelf. On a phone each shelf is a swipeable row of spines (scroll snap); on a wide screen they stack. Every spine is a button with its call number and name, for example "WEB-01 EventFlow", and the featured book is marked.
  - **Book view:** a two-page spread (stacked on a phone). The left is the picture from `images` (with its caption, such as "Concept design"), or a typographic cover when the project has none. The right is the slogan, the **Check out** (live site), **Source** (GitHub) and **Case study** buttons, the problem, what was built and the stack. **Drive to it** appears only when the project has a route stop.
- **Keyboard.** Nothing takes focus when the panel opens. Choosing a book moves focus to its title; Escape (or Back) returns to the shelf and puts focus back on that book; a second Escape leaves the station. "Drive to it" backs the car out of the bay and drives to the project's stop and sign.

## How it works

| Part                     | Where                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stop and landmark        | `millennium-library` in `stops.ts` (kind `section`, 600 m, a control point on the route), `libraryPlacement` in `placement.ts`, `libraryModel.ts`, `MillenniumLibrary.tsx`                                                                                       |
| Station data             | The `projects` entry in `stations.ts` (no `contentRef`: the wall lists books), measured from the stop                                                                                                                                                            |
| Shelves and call numbers | `src/content/library.ts` (shelf labels, order, prefixes WEB / MOB / SYS / AI, the featured project), `category` on each project in `projects.ts`; call numbers are generated by `src/lib/library.ts` (prefix and position on the shelf, published projects only) |
| Logic (pure, tested)     | `src/lib/library.ts`: `buildBooks`, `groupShelves`, `searchBooks`, `findBook`, `bookLinks`                                                                                                                                                                       |
| State                    | `src/store/library.ts`: the open book, the query and the filter; reset whenever the station closes                                                                                                                                                               |
| UI                       | `src/ui/Library/`: `LibraryPanel`, `Catalog`, `Shelf`, `BookView`, `LibraryWall`; `StationWall` shows `LibraryWall` for this station and opens no sheet of its own                                                                                               |

Unpublished projects (`published: false`) have no book and take no call number. The call number order is the order in `projects.ts` within a shelf.

## Content the owner supplies

- **Shelves** (`category` on each project, marked `TODO(content)`): Web has EventFlow, CivicTrack and Game Hub; Mobile has Safe Drive AI and Connexions Spectrum; Systems has the AV Device Controller (Akari is unpublished); AI has RoleReady.
- **Pictures:** see [project-images.md](project-images.md). Game Hub and the AV Device Controller show a typographic cover until they have one. The AV Device Controller gets a real terminal screenshot later.
- **Featured book:** EventFlow, in `library.ts`.

## Cost

The building is four meshes (four draw calls) and one small canvas (the lettering). Measured on the software renderer with the station parked: 43 draw calls on the `low` tier and 64 on `high`, against the 150 budget. The panel is DOM only; the pictures are lazy-loaded when a book opens (not part of the 3D bundle).
