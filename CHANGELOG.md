## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- The route is simplified. Removed the Arcade (Akari), Building under inspection (site audit extension), Warming shelter and CMHR stops (8 stops remain; the Library, Skills and LIILAB stops arrive with their stations). The road after Portage & Main is re-cut (1,123 m to 1,092 m) with long straights at about 600, 700 and 940 m for those stations (`stationSites.test.ts` keeps them straight), the stops' `t` values and the cross streets, building stretches and atmosphere zones are re-anchored (the `shelter` zone is now `residential`; the avenue and Forks zones hang off The Forks until the LIILAB stop exists; the transit map has four zones until then). Akari and the site audit extension are `published: false` and hidden everywhere including `/simple`. The warming shelter links stay on `/simple` only; rule 9 now covers the residential house scenery. The museum and the house become scenery in their own PR
- Drive-by project stops are signs, not popups. The checkpoint panel is gone: each project building carries a lit sign with the project name and a slogan (a new `sign` field on projects, wording marked `TODO(content)`; AV Device Controller has none yet), the radio caption stays and now also announces the sign text for screen readers, and the full project details are in the route map's project list (and, later, the library). Removed `checkpoints.ts`, the panel, `dismissPanel`, and `marqueeSubtitle`; the EventFlow marquee and the CivicTrack request board show the sign wording
- Content synced with mahfuj.dev (owner overrides kept: the email): LIILAB as Junior Software Engineer, Feb 2021 to May 2023; the RRC diploma from August 2023 to August 2025 (graduation TODO removed); a new Industrial Project entry (RRC Polytechnic, mobile app development, May to September 2025); the new Connexions Spectrum project (a library book, no route stop); Safe Drive AI and RoleReady stacks; the CivicTrack repository link; case study links to mahfuj.dev for the projects that have one; and the resume link on mahfuj.dev (`profile.resumeUrl` replaces the local `/resume.pdf`)
- Skills are grouped by category (web, backend, embedded, ai, mobile) instead of dashboard widget groups, with Tailwind, Netlify Functions, React Native and Expo added, `C / C++` split into `C` and `C++`, and `Claude` renamed `Claude Code`

### Added

- Phase 11, launch hardening (`docs/launch.md`): a themed 404 ("Wrong turn, Unit 02"), an Open Graph and Twitter preview image, the favicon and home-screen icons, theme colour, security headers, an updated README with the controls and credits, and a checklist of what only the owner can do (domain, link from mahfuj.dev, device passes, content proofreading).
- Phase 10, the car (`docs/car.md`): the owner's white sedan replaces the placeholder box car: sloped hood and boot, tinted glass cabin, amber roof light bar, black rims, a faint cyan underglow and a "MAHFUJ02" plate (text from `profile.plate`, stylised, not a copy of a real plate). Original primitives, no manufacturer marks, about 30 meshes.
- Phase 9, sound (`docs/sound.md`): a "Sound: off / on" button (off by default, remembered, never autoplays, not even from a saved "on" until the first click or key). Engine hum and tyres on snow follow the car's speed, a wiper swish matches the cabin wipers, and a radio blip plays as a captioned line comes in. Everything is synthesised with the Web Audio API: no audio files, nothing to license, loaded only once sound is on.
- Phase 6, the cabin (`docs/cabin.md`): the dark dashboard edge with the roof light bar glow, a Bangladesh flag, a coffee cup and two windshield wipers that sweep every ten seconds (parked under reduced motion). Decoration only: no text, hidden from assistive tech.
- Phase 8b, the keyboard shortcuts overlay: press `?` (or the round ? button on a wide screen) to list every way to drive; Escape or Close shuts it and gives focus back. Any stop is still at most two clicks away on any device (the route map, a bottom sheet on a phone).
- The continuous patrol loop (`docs/patrol-loop.md`, `docs/uturn.md`), replacing the end-of-shift reset, "New patrol" and Prev: the drive is one closed path that goes on for ever. Out along the road to The Forks, once round the Forks roundabout (the car drives it by itself, no button), back along the return lane visiting every stop in reverse (not Ignition or The Forks) with the stations and signs working from that side too (a second signal mast, the glow mirrored about the stop), then round a small turnaround behind Ignition (a new 30 m of road and a 5-second ring) and out again. Forward input always drives on; backward input brakes the car to a stop and never reverses or turns it. New controls: Brake, Next stop, Turn around (a deliberate U-turn, once) and Start over (fade to the first stop, heading out). Map jumps take the shorter way, turning round at most once. The status line and the route map show the direction ("· outbound" / "· heading back"). Dispatch has a return radio line ("Unit 02, heading back to base.", `radioReturn` on a stop) and the call that starts the patrol is only said on the first lap. Under reduced motion the laps and the U-turn are cuts. The stop positions moved (every `t` shifted by the 30 m) and so did the station sites.
- The ring and the loop are tested end to end: whole-loop sampling for a continuous closed path, a frame-by-frame simulation (forward-only input drives a full loop and comes back outbound, jittery input never turns the car, Turn around turns it once) and position-driven Playwright specs.
- Phase 7b, the Ignition loading screen (`docs/ignition.md`): a dark dashboard with a fuel gauge that fills as the scene loads (a labelled progress bar, and the percentage as text), a Skip to simple view link from the start, and a Start Patrol button when the scene has drawn and its assets are in; Start Patrol fades the screen away (a cut under reduced motion), pulls out to Dispatch and hands focus to Next stop. The page behind is inert and nothing drives the car while it is up; a slow load offers Start Patrol after 25 s. `?intro=off` skips it (every other end-to-end spec uses it). The scene reports progress through `LoadReporter` into `src/store/intro.ts`
- Phase 5h, the Contact station at The Forks (`docs/contact-station.md`): the market building (a long brick hall, a glazed market hall under a snowy gable, a string of warm lights along the eaves) on the right at the end of the route, with the red light, bay and panel of the other stations. The panel lists email, LinkedIn, GitHub, the portfolio at mahfuj.dev and the resume, the same links as `/simple` (shared in `src/lib/contact.ts`). The last 75 m of the road are straightened for it, which lengthens the route from 1,092 m to 1,108 m and shifts every stop's `t`. All five stations are now built; the roundabout, loading screen, sunrise and reset are the rest of Phase 7. Four draw calls for the building
- Phase 5g, the Experience station, the LIILAB office (`docs/experience-station.md`): a small glass office on the right of the route at 940 m on the avenue (a new stop, the route re-pinned there), with a pale roof slab, a canopy, the sign "LIILAB · Sylhet, Bangladesh" in plain lettering and rows of lit monitors behind the glass. The panel lists the LIILAB entry, the Industrial Project and the Sprint-Innovation win in order (`entryRefs` on the station). The avenue atmosphere zone and the transit map's avenue zone now anchor on the LIILAB stop. Five draw calls for the building
- Phase 5f, the Skills station (`docs/skills-station.md`): a tech building on the right of the route at 700 m (a new stop, the route re-pinned there), the first station on the right. The panel lists every skill group with levels as text ("Level 4 of 5", plus pips) and notes in place, and an Also line; the dashboard gauges it replaces stay out of Phase 6. Three draw calls for the building. Landmark and station geometry tests now follow each placement's side and run for every station
- Phase 5e, part 1: the Millennium Library, the Projects station (`docs/library.md`). A new stop at 600 m (the route is re-pinned there), a stylized glass-front building with plain lettering, and the whole interface: a catalog (search plus Web, Mobile, Systems and AI filters, with a live count), a shelf of books with generated call numbers (WEB-01 and so on; swipeable rows on a phone), and a book view with the picture, slogan, Check out, Source, Case study and Drive to it. Shelves are the new `category` on projects and `src/content/library.ts`. Four draw calls for the building; 43 draw calls on `low` and 64 on `high` with the station parked. The 3D interior is part 2
- Project images (`docs/project-images.md`) for the Millennium Library books: real screenshots of EventFlow and RoleReady (1280x800 WebP, about 55 KB and 22 KB), and the owner's Safe Drive AI and Connexions Spectrum concept designs (captioned "Concept design"); Game Hub and the AV Device Controller keep a typographic cover. The AI-tool watermark in the corner of the two concept images is painted out by the prepare script. Two scripts, `scripts/capture-project-screenshots.mjs` and `scripts/prepare-project-images.mjs` (WebP, quality about 80, at most 1280 px wide or 900 px tall, at most 150 KB each, total 134 KB). The project schema's `images` gained an optional `caption` and a check that the path is a WebP in `public/images/projects/`
- Scenery (`docs/scenery.md`): lit buildings with no stop and no panel. A warm-windowed house on the residential street (warm windows only, no figures, no text), the Canadian Museum for Human Rights (a stone base and a tall glowing glass tower, no lettering) and Esplanade Riel (a footbridge with a glowing spire and cable stays) on the avenue. About seven draw calls; the `Landmark` base gained `minGlow` and `lean` options so scenery is always lit and never pulls the camera
- Phase 5d, stop 6 (Portage & Main, RoleReady): a glass office tower on a limestone podium just past the intersection, with a lit sign board on its front showing the project name, its slogan and two speech bubbles (an interview question and an answer). Four meshes, four draw calls, one small canvas. Wording from the project's `sign` in `projects.ts`; the radio caption is unchanged and the sign text is announced beside it
- Phase 5d, stop 5 (City services building, CivicTrack): a limestone civic hall with a columned entrance, a request board over the door that names the project and shows the three stages a request moves through, rooftop lettering ("City Services", our own type, no city logo or crest) and a lower brick annex; five meshes, so five draw calls, no new textures beyond two small canvases. It opens the CivicTrack panel with its links and a radio caption. Wording is in `signage.ts`, `radio.ts` and `checkpoints.ts`
- Phase 5c, the transit-map menu (`docs/routemap.md`): the route as a vertical line coloured by zone with stops, ringed station entries, a live "you are here" car, section buttons, the project list with a details panel (and "Drive to it" for projects with a stop), the resume and contact links, and a live status line. A left panel on wide screens and a bottom sheet on a phone; keyboard accessible; DOM only, so no draw-call change. Zones are in `src/content/transit.ts`; choosing a station from the map drives there and opens it on arrival (`goToStation`)
- Phase 5b, the station arrival pattern, with the Education station on the RRC campus: a station light that turns red as the car arrives and an Open Education button (the red-light pause never blocks driving on); the car turns into a bay in front of the campus, the camera swings in behind it, the headlights light the wall, and the content fades in as real DOM placed on the wall by a CSS 3D transform; Escape, the Leave button or driving on backs the car out (reduced motion is a cut, and a small screen opens the content in the bottom sheet too). Per-station data in `src/content/stations.ts`; a pure state machine and parking and wall maths in `src/lib`; `docs/stations.md`
- The RRC campus is a station now, so its side panel is replaced by the wall; the project stops keep the side panel
